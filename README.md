## A little about me

I build thoughtful software at the intersection of **AI**, **human development**, and **calm technology**. I am especially interested in systems that make powerful ideas easier to understand, explore, and use.

- Building [Agent Conductor](https://github.com/mettafive/agent-conductor), a visible and independently checked workflow for AI agents

- Creating [Zen](https://github.com/mettafive/zen), a mindfulness companion for macOS
